## About me

I am an Electronic Engineer and M.Sc. in Electronic and Computer Engineering, currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Engineering at **Universidad de los Andes** in Bogotá, Colombia.

My work is centered on **control systems, robotics, modeling, simulation, embedded platforms, and real-world robotic implementation**. I am a research member of the **GIAP Research Group** at Universidad de los Andes and I develop research and teaching tools that connect mathematical models, simulation environments, control algorithms, and physical robotic systems.

I am particularly interested in building **reproducible control-oriented workflows** that make it easier to move from modeling and simulation to experimentation and hardware deployment.

> 🌐 A more complete academic portfolio, including projects, publications, education, and experience, is available on my **[GitHub Pages academic website](https://leffertrochez.github.io/)**.

## Featured work

### 🤖 Sim2Real Control Framework for Unitree G1

**A MATLAB/Simulink-Based Sim2Real Control Framework for the Unitree G1 Using ROS 2 and MuJoCo**

A unified model-based environment for implementing, validating, debugging, and transferring humanoid control strategies from simulation to the real Unitree G1 robot.
